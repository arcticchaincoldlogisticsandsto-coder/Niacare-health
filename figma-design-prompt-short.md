NiaCare: professional healthcare app for Tanzania (patients, doctors, admins). Style: Stripe/Linear-level, calm and clinical, not a bright consumer app.

Rules: primary blue #0057B8, one teal accent used sparingly, status colors (green/amber/red) only for status not decoration. No purple/pink/cyan tiles. Plus Jakarta Sans font, restrained weights (no max-bold everywhere). 8-12px radius, subtle shadows. Lucide-style icons only, zero emoji. Minimal motion, no bounce/pulse except active emergency dispatch. Light + dark mode.

Screens: landing, login/OTP, health-passport card, patient dashboard, emergency dispatch, appointments, records, prescriptions, billing, admin console.
